Problem 1 — parser — 379 of 412 reports

The shared parser reads every date month-first, so tenants whose locale writes day-first get day and month flipped. All 11 tenants on file are day-first (de-DE, es-ES, en-GB, nl-NL, cs-CZ, it-IT, fr-FR, pl-PL, pt-PT, da-DK, en-IE), so all of them are affected outside the booking-v3 form: app (204 reports), email (150), booking-v2 form (25). First seen 2026-04-01, last seen 2026-10-04. Intake tagged 356 of these parser and 23 hint; the computed cause disagrees with the tag for 23 reports. Desk notes read 5 of them as a one-day move (R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620), filed under this cause.

Filed as a ticket in this repo: https://github.com/Bumshakalaka/s01e03-what-it-shows/issues/1

Problem 2 — hint — 33 of 412 reports

The booking-v3 form prints an MM/DD/YYYY hint under the date field. The tenant's staff type what the hint tells them, month first — but the form parses the entry the tenant's day-first way, flipping day and month. A different bug from parser, and only the form channel on booking-v3 tenants is affected: alder.physio (12 reports), copper.studio (11), pale.harbour (10). First seen 2026-04-17, last seen 2026-09-22. Intake tagged 18 of these hint and 15 parser.

Filed as a ticket in this repo: https://github.com/Bumshakalaka/s01e03-what-it-shows/issues/2
